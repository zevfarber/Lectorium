Lectorium

A complete reading suite for the world’s classic myths, legends and wisdom, in their original languages.

You have heard of the Odyssey all your life. These are its first two lines as Homer composed them. Tap any word to see what it means. Tap Listen to hear them.

planchthē, epei Troiēs hieron ptoliethron epersen·

Why I built this

I love to experience the magic of classic cultural writings in their original languages, but to become fluent in all these languages and scripts is impossible.

So for most of my life, when I wanted to read an ancient text, I had to find a dictionary, a grammar, and a translation and work through it with all three open. It was insanely slow and I would get stuck anyway: I couldn’t see how the translation came out of the words, I had no one to ask, and I had little idea how the text should sound.

That’s why I built Lectorium, which puts all of that into one package, the way an office suite puts all your programs in one place. This program lets anyone, from the total beginner to the almost fluent, experience these texts in their original with minimal friction. Try it out and explore the world’s myths, legends, and wisdom sayings with nothing but your phone or computer.

Zev Farber

Suggest a correction

Lectorium's texts are prepared with care but are the best effort of a machine, not a proofreading by a native reader — so errors are expected, and pointing them out is how they get fixed. Your note goes to the project; each text is corrected once, for everyone.
